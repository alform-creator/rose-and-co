What makes a signature arrangement distinctive

Basket, box or classic bouquet

Each presentation has a different character. Baskets feel abundant, flower boxes look neat and contemporary, and a hand-tied bouquet remains a versatile choice.

Look beyond familiar colours

Blue, purple, peach and cream accents can give an arrangement a unique personality. Choose a palette that fits the moment and the recipient.
