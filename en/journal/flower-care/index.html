Bouquet Care: How to Keep Flowers Fresh

Fresh water, a clean cut and a few simple habits can help your flowers last longer.

Preparing Your Bouquet for a Vase

Remove leaves that would sit below the waterline, and trim the stems using a clean cutting tool.

How to Keep Cut Flowers Fresh Longer

Refresh the water frequently, wash the vase, and keep blooms away from direct sun, heat and ripening fruit.

Different flowers have different needs; ask your florist if you are unsure.
